- `natBad_pct` = fraction with a nature that **lowers the mon's main attack stat** (the clear failure mode — should stay low).
- **Flags:** realized OVER intended by >0.12 ⇒ 🔴 over-parity; UNDER by >0.12 ⇒ 🔵 under-delivered.

## Findings
- **Items respect the player-parity (Battle-Dojo) clock — the one strict invariant:** at C0-1 (intent tier 0) realized item% = 0% (must be ~0 — foe can't out-tier the player), rising to 78% late. This is the cleanest signal and it holds.
- **Nature failures are low once judged by moveset** (not base stats): mean natBad% = 4% (a nature lowering the stat the mon actually attacks with). Any stage over 15% is flagged in the tables.
- **Ability & nature optimization ramp up as intended** (descriptive — the outcome undercounts the dialed intent): abilAlt% (ability upgraded off slot-0) 6%→40% early→late; hidden-ability use reaches 17% late. natOpt% 12%→73%. (Many mons' best ability/nature IS the default, so 100% is not the expected ceiling.)
- **Movesets are rich and scale correctly:** late/endgame mean coverage 2.36 distinct attacking types, avgBP 92.91, STAB 1.48 per slot; early coverage 2.4, avgBP 82.66 (early BP intentionally held down by the foe BP cap + basic-STAB stage). Status-move presence 73% late.
- **Parity/quality flags (of 192 stage×gen rows):** 0 item-before-parity (foe holds an item the player can't yet), 0 with >15% bad-nature rate, 0 late stages with <85% STAB coverage.

> **Scope note:** this measures kit *composition* (what moves/ability/item/nature each foe carries), not in-battle *execution* (the AI's move selection). It also does not model the player's realized kit — it leans on the engine's parity design (foe knobs are tuned ≤ player availability per city), which the item clock confirms is honoured.
