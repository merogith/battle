The honest baseline

01What's already good — and what actually reads as "indie"

You can't plan an upgrade without being fair about the floor. This build is not emoji-and-divs; it has a mature visual spine. The tell that it isn't a shipped Pokémon game lives in a short, fixable list.

Already strong — protect these

  • Animated battlers. Player/foe/party/box/HoF mons render as Showdown gen5ani GIFs, local-bundled with remote fallback. getSprite()
  • Real design tokens. A :root layer with motion vars, category-coded facility hues, radius/spacing ramps, AA-compliant grays.
  • Cinematic layer exists. window.StoryFx already does encounter reveal, ball throw, evolution scene, HoF confetti, VS splash.
  • Accessibility spine. 30+ prefers-reduced-motion blocks + JS gating that caps delays and no-ops particles.
  • One deliberate typeface. Self-hosted Press Start 2P (OFL) — the retro identity is a choice, not an accident.

Reads as indie — the target list

  • Flat send-outs. Mons pop in; they don't burst from a thrown ball. Battle start lacks the first-party ritual.
  • Weightless hits. No attack lunge, hit-shake, damage flash, or faint fade — turns lack impact feedback.
  • Static NPC faces. In-city dialogue is one static PNG; the emotion system only lives in the full-screen narrative overlay.
  • Emoji rewards. Badges are 🏅; level-ups are a bare toast. The big payoff moments under-deliver.
  • No wayfinding. Cities are static hubs — legible, but there's no map/route texture to sell a journey.

Sequenced delivery

04The plan — five phases, feel-first

Ordered by impact-per-effort and by risk to the sensitive Story-flow/save code (which none of this should touch — it's presentation only). Each phase is independently shippable and leaves a reduced-motion path intact.

P0

Motion & design-system tightening ~1 day low risk

Formalize the vocabulary everything else draws on, so later phases are consistent by construction.

  • Add an explicit easing + duration token scale (entrance ease-out-quart, exit ease-in, micro 120ms / screen 320ms / cinematic beats skippable).
  • Audit all animations to transform/opacity only — compositor-driven, 60fps, no layout thrash.
  • Adopt duiker101 type SVGs + Showdown category icons as shared components (retire text type-chips).
P1

Battle feel ~3–4 days med risk

The highest-impact phase. Give every turn weight — this is what most separates it from a first-party title.

  • Send-out ritual — ball throw → burst → mon materialize (mirror your existing catch FX).
  • Hit feedback — attacker lunge, target shake + white damage-flash, super-effective screen micro-shake, faint fade-drop.
  • HP & status polish — eased HP tick-down with counter, status-inflict flash, type-effectiveness banner sweep.
P2

Expressive dialogue ~3–5 days med risk

Turn characters from nameplates into faces. Biggest narrative uplift, and it closes the in-city vs overlay inconsistency.

  • Adopt PMD portrait sets for professor, rival, mystery figure, and facility NPCs — per-line emotion swaps.
  • Unify the two dialogue presenters on one emotion-driven component; retire the static in-city PNG.
  • Typewriter + nameplate — authentic Pokémon dialogue face, tap-to-complete, animated nameplate, cinematic letterbox.
P3

Reward & progression moments ~2–3 days low risk

Make the payoffs land. These are the screenshots players share.

  • Real 8 gym badges + badge case with shine-sweep + pin-to-case reveal and the official jingle — retire the 🏅 emoji.
  • Level-up / stat-gain panel with arrow-up stat rows + the authentic level-up jingle, replacing the toast.
  • Catch & evolution polish — "Gotcha!" celebration; extend the existing evolution scene with a light burst, cry + evolution fanfare.
P4

World, wayfinding & atmosphere ~4–6 days bigger

Sell the journey and tie the whole thing together. Largest scope — split or defer if time-boxed.

  • City key-art + route strip — per-city banner and a position-on-journey map built from pret tiles (hub model preserved).
  • Gen-5 VS-bar wipe into battle intros; biome-consistent official backdrops per city.
  • Official soundtrack pass — real battle/route/gym/victory BGM + jingles wired through AudioSystem, per-biome particles, transition wipes, final a11y + perf sweep (GIF weight, concurrent-animation cap).

Animation principles every phase honors

Compositor-only motionAnimate transform & opacity; never width/top/left. 60fps or it doesn't ship.
Duration disciplineMicro-interactions 100–200ms; screen transitions 300–500ms; cinematic beats always skippable.
Reduced-motion is a fallback, not a deleteSwap slides for fades, cut cinematics to instant — keep meaning. Extend your existing 30+ blocks.
Seeded, never randomAny randomized visual (shakes, wobble) uses storyRngNext — deterministic replays are the product.
No flash > 3×/secPhotosensitivity floor — glitch/flash effects (mystery figure) stay under the threshold.
Bundle local, fall back remoteFollow the existing sprite pattern: ship assets locally, degrade to Showdown/PokéAPI, then placeholder.
Before any of this ships

05Guardrails & open decisions

This plan is presentation-only by design. With licensing off the table (private project), the remaining gates are code-discipline and taste — three things to keep in view.

Story flow & saves stay untouched

Every item here is visual — no damage formula, AI, RNG semantics, or save-schema change. That keeps this clear of the codebase's most sensitive area. Per CLAUDE.md, even so, visual changes still need your sign-off before commit.

Licensing — cleared, so mix freely

Private project: official first-party rips, community art (Showdown / PMD), and CC0 (Kenney / OGA) can all be used directly. The only remaining care is consistency — don't mix a gen-3 backdrop with gen-5 battlers and a gen-4 HUD. Pick one visual era per surface and hold it.

Performance budget

Weight is now the real limiter: the monolith is ~4MB, gen5ani GIFs are heavy, and adding real BGM/backdrops compounds it. Keep new motion CSS/compositor-side, lazy-load offscreen sprites & audio, cap concurrent animations, prefer APNG/WebP + compressed audio, and watch the sloppy-mode global-leak hazard when adding loader state.
