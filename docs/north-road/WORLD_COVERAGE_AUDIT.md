# Battle v2.0.4 world and interface coverage audit

[Art and production direction](PRESENTATION.md#6-world-presentation-plan) · [Campaign design](CAMPAIGN.md) · [Release and device verification](RELEASE.md) · [Implementation runbook and asset kit](WORLD_UPGRADE_RUNBOOK.md)

The biggest immersion gap is continuity between screens. The game already has consequences, unlocks, sightings and recurring people. A player often sees those as another notice or panel, while the route, town, local services and battle use different visual and audio cues. Prioritize places that are recognizable before, during and after a decision. A distinctive town, an easy-to-find service and a partner that visibly responds will improve more of the playthrough than another set of menus or one more isolated cinematic.

This is an audit of commit `5a05e63` and the work already in v2.0.4. It follows the application entry points, story and encounter catalogs, runtime gates, fallback render paths, save data and tests. The code findings and proposed changes below are separate from the browser evidence at the end. The unit of coverage is a player interaction or an authored content record. Data-driven combinations such as species/forms, legal builds and seeded trainer assignments are covered by a family of assertions and representative fixtures.

### Priorities before the art pass

1. **Fix known presentation and guidance defects first.** Restore the Breakwater departure trigger, use Victory Road artwork for its three trainer entrances, and correct the backup version label. Reconcile conflicting service gates, unattached content and release tests that still expect old copy. Verify resume and update safety as each affected flow changes.
2. **Build one loop with shared UI and media.** Use Pallet → Breakwater → Flintreach → Center/Bag/Dex → first Gym → town return. Give the current action a stable place on small screens. Use existing choice records so the route’s consequence is visible in Flintreach. Include one companion interaction and a transition into and out of a local battle.
3. **Roll out by density and tone.** Use the same components in a crowded service hub such as Glasswater and a quiet late location such as Beaconrest/Cathedral before updating every other location. Give rituals such as Gym check-in, a healed party, a rare catch and a Hall of Fame entry their own recognizable conclusion.
4. **Preserve the working rules while improving presentation.** Compare saves, economy, progression gates, battle results, legal choices and exact resumes against the baseline. Document every intended change to an existing rule or unlock if the design later calls for one.
