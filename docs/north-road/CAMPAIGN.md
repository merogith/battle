## Fixed identifiers

- Nine road stories save one `scout | help | pass` value under `storyChoices['road.discovery.' + cityIdx]`. The town number matches the road ordinal. Roads 1–8 have optional first-search habitat preferences. Victory Road Scout has no wild encounter.
- Keep town indices for geographic and facility gates in Pallet, Flintreach, Kettlestone, Lindenbell, Reedmarket, Glasswater, Brasslock, Beaconrest, Vespergate and Pokémon League order. One resolver supplies city, arrival, departure, menu, archive and Pokédex labels. Preserve the seeded leader’s canonical hometown in a labelled visitor origin.
- Open Circle has ten IDs, `chapter-1` through `chapter-9` and `aftermath`, and two values, `listen` and `act`. Existing evidence, echoes and replay predicates remain authoritative.
- Each villain track has 11 beats. Battle 1, battle 2, mini-boss and boss use Road 4, 5, 6 and 7 respectively, with setup beginning on Road 2 and aftermath on Road 8. The 10 tracks total 110 scene keys and 40 fight entries.
- Each anomaly track has 10 beats. Its three fights are `miniRaid` on Road 4, `miniRaid2` on Road 5 and `raid` on Road 6. Setup begins on Road 1 and aftermath uses Road 7. The eight tracks total 80 scene keys and 24 battle entries. Road labels are earliest narration anchors. Some beats spill to later eligible route battle rows.
