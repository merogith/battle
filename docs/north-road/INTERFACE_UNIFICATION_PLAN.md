# Battle interface unification plan

**Baseline** · Live Battle 2.0.5, game commit `6f1716f`, with release test follow-up `c0d54b5`. This plan covers the handoff between the newer North Road presentation and the older interface. Its unit of work is a complete player task, from where the player finds it to where they return. Visiting a screen once counts as evidence that it opens. Each applicable state and interaction still needs its own result. [Release changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) · [World coverage inventory](WORLD_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md) · [World upgrade runbook](WORLD_UPGRADE_RUNBOOK.md)

Start with the independent coverage review and its P0 correctness fixes. It reconciles the 2.0.5 register, verifies the source and release inventories, and defines the additional save, engine, artistic and replay checks required before rollout. [Coverage review and implementation gates](INTERFACE_COVERAGE_REVIEW.md)

Review the controls, copy, feedback, art and runtime handlers inside each surface against the detail audit. Use its keep, combine, show-when-needed and remove decisions as the starting point for each migration. Resolve the story source ownership before regenerating or rewriting scenes. [Detail review](INTERFACE_DETAIL_REVIEW.md)

## What the review found

The town hub is the best reference for the next pass. Beaconrest gives the player a place, a short objective and a direct action. Step into the Pokémart and the text changes to a wide pixel face, most product descriptions expand by default and each item acquires another box. Move Tutor and Battle Mentor combine the new backdrops and cleaner search with different frames, fonts and price treatments. Storage, Bag and Notebook use three visual treatments for the same party and its supplies. The busy service areas need a shared layout that still gives each NPC a recognizable place to work. [Town styling](../../world-location.css#L195) · [Service markup](../../battle.html#L11191) · [Older facility styles](../../battle.html#L2254) · [Facility overrides](../../game-upgrades.css#L1731)

The split continues outside towns. Open Circle chapters have a readable scene, heading and action footer. Some road narration and story minigames switch to small floating boxes and old text treatments over stretched art. Quick Draft uses broad retro cards, the main battle field has its own compact HUD, and Competitive Arena has independent page styles. A single control language across these areas would make the changes of pace and place feel deliberate. [Chapter UI](../../data/story/adventure.js#L148) · [Narration UI](../../battle.html#L62751) · [Draft UI](../../battle.html#L10633) · [Battle UI](../../battle.html#L11871) · [Arena styles](../../competitive/arena.css)

The main HTML ships roughly **10,300 lines of embedded CSS** with **523 `!important` declarations**, followed by external theme and feature stylesheets. Broad selectors still style entire facility trees and dynamic templates add inline declarations. That is how newer header rules can coexist with old buttons, text and dialogs within the same view. A scoped migration must account for the actual winning declarations at each breakpoint. Unlayered rules and existing important rules need explicit treatment before adopting cascade layers. ## Target style and interaction rules

1. **Use North Road as the reference.** Draw the frame from the dark, matte town and chapter panels. Use a warm primary action and a stable neutral surface. Use color for meaning such as type, success, danger or a selected item. Let the scene art, sign, portrait and one local cue give a room its identity. Keep the Game Corner's felt, the Frontier's challenge seals and a Gym's team colors within that frame.
2. **Give the player one clear starting action.** A header gives place and Back. Use currency or party count only where it affects the next decision. For a Mart, show item name, short effect and cost. For a tutor, show the selected partner, one legal suggestion and direct access to the complete list. At a major fork, give each real choice equal footing and say what pressing it commits.
3. **Use two typography roles across the whole game.** Carry the readable in-game face through labels, instructions, prices, controls and dialogue. Keep the existing pixel face for signs, venue names where it fits, badges and occasional short display accents. Give recurring tools one action vocabulary, one price format and consistent type, category and stat cues. Compare purpose and rhythm with the training and battle flows in Pokémon Champions, and let the handheld influence come through in compact choices and recognizable places. [Pokémon Champions gameplay](https://champions.pokemon.com/en-us/gameplay/)
4. **Keep meaningful detail close to the decision.** Expose effects, filters, exact sets, history and comparisons through the existing inspect or disclosure route. Show the current partner, cost or resource, relevant consequence and next action before commitment. Keep warnings for release, trade, sales, wagers and run replacement visible at the point of commitment. Ordinary conversation can start with a single natural line. Leave room for long story scenes when the player chooses to read them.
5. **Give every exit a destination.** Preserve the origin, active partner, search and filters, selected row, scroll and focus on return. Use stable IDs so a row can move or evolve. Back from a shop opened by a voucher returns to that Bag context. Back from an encounter's Pokédex entry returns to the pending encounter. Browser Back, Escape, visible Back and touch should follow the same navigation contract.
6. **Use the same controls in every supported layout.** Aim for 44×44 CSS pixels for routine touch actions. Test regular-text contrast against 4.5:1, large text against 3:1, and meaningful controls and focus against applicable non-text contrast guidance. At large text use natural wrapping and in-flow actions where necessary. In short landscape place art beside the task or use a shallow crop. Put no critical information solely in color, audio, hover or animation. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) · [Target size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-enhanced) · [Focus and overlays](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum)

## Definition of done

- **Coverage is measurable.** Each registered screen, dialog, dynamic overlay, place, story family and supported mode has an assigned check. A surface entry records the visible first objective, actions, transaction ownership, exit, focus, normal and compact screenshots, and applicable failure or interruption. Each conditional gate has a fixture or negative test. For combinatorial content, catalog validation exhausts IDs, references and availability. Real journeys test selected branches, pacing and transitions using the same stable seeds before and after.
- **The default view is easy to use.** A new player can find the next action, select the Pokémon or item, verify what will change, and get back without opening Help. An experienced player can still inspect the exact rules and filter results on the same path. Prices, partner names, choices and focused actions remain visible. No content or controls overlap, clip or leave focus on the page body during the supported flows. Use contrast, touch size, text scale, hit tests, focus restoration and screenshot diffs to complement transaction assertions.
- **The game rules survive the presentation changes.** Compare save and battle event snapshots for identical inputs. Verify badges, money, purchased and sold items, grades, move legality and training ceilings, generation and mechanic restrictions, starter bond, archive and unlock flags, story choices and seeded opponent/wild/offer identity. Cover cancel, duplicate click, retry, reload, interrupt, replace/import save, offline Continue, both offline pack profiles, network refusal, and the supported exact-turn update. Check hosted rooms with independent clients once staging is available.
- **The build stays light.** Measure actual encoded bytes and cold/warm p75 with the same throttling and fresh browser profiles as the baseline. Keep the public title below the existing 1 MiB budget and first native action below 5 MiB. Keep Compact within the existing 80 MiB target and measure update delta separately. Require repeat navigation and a long native battle to show bounded listeners, timers, canvases, media, logs and pending requests. Add the new styles to verified manifests and test a broken primary image, offline reads and update activation before release. [Release procedures](RELEASE.md)
- **Run the full release gate.** Use `player-facilities-playtest.mjs` for the full 16-service pass in both portrait and short landscape, plus dedicated disabled and full-capacity states. Run `player-playtest.mjs` for the packaged campaign, stateful checks for transactions, both chapter outcomes, all Camp games, side activities, Arena, battle motion, responsive UI, notifications, keyboard navigation, retry/resume and offline tests. Add the high-value screenshot and focus assertions to those existing harnesses. Run Chromium, WebKit and Firefox through the packaged Pages workflow, then verify the live version and upgrade on physical devices. Mark separately what first-time players say about comprehension and immersion in an uncoached playtest.
