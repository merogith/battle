# Battles and competitive rules

Use this reference when a task says “all battles,” “all modes,” “battle screen on mobile,” “generation rules,” or “enemy builds.” Include each appropriate entry path below, from the first decision to re-entry after the result. Story route placement and unlock timing live in the world reference. Party, Bag and Pokémon Summary have their own field tools reference. [WORLD.md](WORLD.md) · [FIELD_TOOLS.md](FIELD_TOOLS.md)

A generation selection in the Classic battle options filters when a species or form debuted. The shared Classic board runs mixed-generation singles. Use Competitive Arena for generation-specific competitive rules. `getDraftPool` prefers authored builds, then Gen 9 random builds, then species data if necessary. It excludes directly draftable Mega/Primal and G-Max forms. A draft rolls one eligible form per species, applies role/item/weather/type balancing where enabled, and keeps both offers unique across sides. Check the final generated pools, since Smart Pool can replace candidates after the initial draw. [Pool selection](../../battle.html#L13358) · [Draft assembly](../../battle.html#L21494)

## Competitive Arena

- **Setup** — Gen 1–9 OU, each as 3v3 or 6v6, plus Gen 8 Battle Stadium Singles as 3v3 with level 50 and Dynamax/G-Max. Expand preview of preset teams, optionally paste a Showdown export or enter a `0`–`4294967295` seed, then start. Cover team length mismatch, rules validation, unsupported seed and offline/worker errors. [Setup markup](../../competitive/index.html) · [Format, validation and roster rules](../../scripts/competitive/arena-simulation.mjs)
- **Battle** — Choose a lead, then legal moves and switches, forced replace, type and status badges, HP, current turn and weather, expandable teams and battle events, move details including priority and accuracy, legal generation-specific battle form toggles, speed/reduced motion and Skip. Include Gens 1–2 rules and build validation, Gen 7 Mega/Z, Gen 8 Stadium Max, Gen 9 Tera, disabled PP and stale action recovery. [Arena UI](../../competitive/arena.js) · [Arena shell and layout](../../competitive/arena.css)
- **Exit and replay** — Win/loss/draw, New Battle, reset/end and return, seed and structured event log. Download replay JSON contains the simulator version, format, seed and battle data. Keep the download separate from a native Story save or an online rematch. Worker load begins on demand and failures return to setup with a message. [Arena client](../../competitive/arena-client.js) · [Replay creation](../../scripts/competitive/arena-simulation.mjs#L397) · [Worker](../../scripts/competitive/showdown-worker.mjs)
