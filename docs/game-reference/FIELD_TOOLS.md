# Field tools and Pokémon details

Use this guide for changes to the Bag, Pokémon cards, party or PC, every Pokémon Summary, the Pokédex and its neighboring archives, or the tools that tell players where to go. The IDs match [the scope index](scopes.json). [Services and their local entry points](FACILITIES.md), [activities](ACTIVITIES.md), [battle modes](BATTLES.md), [the journey](WORLD.md), and [the app and accessibility controls](APP.md) carry their own gates and rules.

A full pass follows the normal entry point in each context, opens optional details, checks the smallest layout, performs each available action and one representative rejected action, then returns to the original screen and control. Browse a populated save as well as a fresh or empty one. Look for disclosure text that repeats its label, unexplained locks, duplicate routes to an action, stale results, and a back button whose destination changes unexpectedly. Keep the current purpose and safe next action visible. Put counts, technical detail and advice behind the disclosure or tab players expect to contain them.

## party-summary — Party, cards, every Pokémon Summary

### Party and battle switch selector

- The field Party (`#modal-story-party`, `openPartyModal`, `renderTeamPanel`) shows lead and order, grade, actual battle-stat total and role, health/status and space. It distinguishes starter, favorite, cosmetic form, shiny and bound partner. Eggs open the incubation information. The Arrange disclosure reveals pointer drag, directional reorder and Set as lead. Check no-op boundaries, lead eligibility and persistence on reload. An egg occupies its own slot and is not a fighter.
- The battle Party (`#modal-party`, `openParty`, `selectPartyMember`) has the correct actor’s roster, current/fainted marker, current HP and status, labeled Summary and Switch. Voluntary switches can cancel. Trapping, a KO replacement and mid-move pivots have separate legal choices and prompts. Hazards on the switching side remain visible where applicable. Online forced choices expose the match-forfeit path. The Summary action keeps the fight paused and should return to the exact inspected button.
- Exercise via the battle command button and keyboard, then via actual KO and pivot flows. Verify the active slot after a fast and a slow sprite load, fainted Pokémon, trapped slot, dragged lead, and hot-seat or online actor change. The switch prompts and replacement sequencing are part of the battle contract in [BATTLES.md](BATTLES.md).

| Draft offer, reserve, one-off and league previews | `showDraftPokemonSummary` accepts one build or an ordered caller-supplied list. Audit all three `#modal-summary` tabs, the persistent sprite/name/types header, and close, previous/next, and optional Story reorder controls.

- **Overview** has the current item and consumed or removed item state when known, ability, nature, level, status, HP, base displayed combat stats, total including HP, stage and effective-stat information in battle, and role notes in Story. In opted-in training views, check IVs, EVs, bond effects and any applicable Story scaling. The in-Party EV focus control has Auto and six choices. It preserves focus and announces the state after success. Keep source and display in agreement if a save fails.
- **Moves** has one to four equipped moves, type/category icons, power, accuracy, PP, descriptions and applicable live restrictions. Check base and Z/Max previews when applicable, unavailable mechanics, Hidden Power typing, disabled/locked moves and a missing illustration. Previewed Z/Max details do not change the actual equipped moves.
- **Matchups** has defense types, weaknesses, resistances, immunities and context-sensitive battle information such as coverage, hazards or field effects when relevant. Check both types and actual live form, transformed/Tera state, and unavailable or hidden information.
- The header and page tabs stay reachable in short landscape. Check tab keys and scroll position, sprite fallback, and Escape/corner/visible Close. Returning to Party or PC preserves the row and exact control where available. The original context lives beneath the Summary and should not accept focus while the Summary is active.

## center-storage — Pokémon Center, PC and Underground

`#screen-story-pokemoncenter` starts on Storage. `enterPokemonCenter`, `_pcRefresh` and `pcSwitchTab` own the entry, gold, ball counts, seen/caught figures, Party/PC capacity, and local tabs. Nurse Joy’s first visit has a one-time tutorial gift. Healing at encounter boundaries follows Story rules. For first introduction, current-city availability and facility Back, also check [FACILITIES.md](FACILITIES.md).

| Feature | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| Party and PC Box | Live Party and reserve cards, party gate, 30-slot PC cap, warning at 24, empty and full zones, permanent leading-fighter minimum, packed party vs. egg withdrawal, depositing the last fighter or a favorite, unavailable controls and text. Check duplicate species and stable instance IDs. Pointer and keyboard reordering, drag across boxes, insertion position, failed save, focus after the row is replaced, and stored order all matter. |
| Search, sort and display | Box search by nickname or species, revealed Sort and filter, sorting by order caught, Dex number, grade, alphabetical name, type, base stats or actual battle-stat total, type filter, Shinies only, compound no-results and Clear filters. `_pcBoxView` only changes the visible set. | Inspection and removal | Summary opens for individual Pokémon and incubation information opens for eggs. Check protected/unsellable slots, the last fighter, capacity, cancelled irreversible actions, proceeds, failed writes, and the result on reload. |
| Underground | `_pcRenderUndergroundTab` and its activity functions own the sell-only roster and its prices. ## Context, layout and failure checks for future changes

1. Start at Home, a town before and after the first Badge, a town with multiple service paths, a Camp return, the Crucible, a Catch encounter and a Classic battle. Follow each relevant tool's opening link.
2. Test fresh, legacy/imported, populated, limited-capacity and deliberately rejected states. Verify progress after a real round trip through the persistence path where updates are expected. Compare a seed-controlled journey with and without opening read-only dialogs.
3. Test browser Back, app Back, Escape, Close, tools opened above other tools, keyboard exit and focus return, modal scrolling and tap targets. Compare desktop, tablet, portrait phone and short landscape, with readable font, large text and reduced motion on a sample. For network loss, verify sprite fallback and text/labels remain useful.
4. When changing records or data, make one real action that creates each affected record or state and confirm it appears exactly once. Report whether the evidence came from a player-path browser test, a synthetic preview, a focused data/DOM test, or source review. Maintain the scope registry and existing playtest coverage register when adding or removing a surface.
