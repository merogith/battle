## Camp and bonding

**Entry and navigation.** `_campIsDueForTransition` and `enterCamp` sit in the Story route progression. Camp can follow consecutive regular route fights or a route transition. The first catch tutorial and its result precede the early Camp offer. Gym and League sequences retain their own spacing. `campByEventIdx` records destination visits. `campReturnPoint` sends a player who returns to the previous town back to the same camp before continuing. Camp grass uses its own Wander budget entry keyed by `100000 + destination index`. It uses regular balls and returns through the same camp after the capture flow.

**Screen states.** `#story-camp-scene` moves through the `camp-menu`, `camp-roster`, `camp-mon`, `camp-ready` and `camp-result` panels. The menu offers Spend time with your team, Search the tall grass where available, Head back to the previous town and Break camp. A roster row opens that partner's activities and a Bond progress disclosure. `Begin activity` follows the player's timing preference. `Play without a timer` or `Play with a timer` changes it for that activity. The timed path has `#camp-countdown` and `#camp-microgame`, with Leave activity on `data-camp-exit`. The quieter **Spend time together** path gives the same eligible bond advance. It has its own Cancel activity and dismissal path. Success, miss and leaving a game have distinct results. Check game cancellation, return focus and the next screen together.

**Rules and reward.** Fresh bond counters start at 1 out of 5. All six paths master at 5. A successful action gives one mark or two for the path associated with a nature's raised stat. Devotion/HP has no nature preference. Mastery grants +5% to the path's stat, recalculated for the Pokémon when its build is used. Bond counts, partner title and the first mastery receipt belong to the individual slot. A failed write leaves progress unearned and the reward can be retried. A miss, cancellation or leaving produces no mark. Difficulty within a game scales with the partner's progress through the path using `_CT` and `_campEffectiveGame`.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Cheer `cheer` | Praise / Attack | `clap` timed tap, `pump` mash, `pose` matching, `combo` sequence, `highfive` stop gauge |
| Feed & Groom `feed` | Nurture / Sp. Atk | `feed` lob timing, `brush` swipe coverage, `catch` basket, `groom` steady hold, `soothe` trace |
| Drill `drill` | Discipline / Defense | `anchor` balance, `brace` block, `plant` steady hold, `march` mash, `stack` drop alignment |

| Stargaze `stargaze` | Devotion / HP | `holdclose` hold and release, `whisper` drag aim, `gaze` tracking, `embrace` balance, `pulse` stop gauge |

`CAMP_TITLES` checks for Soulbonded first (all six), then the Obsession (Devotion with Discipline or Intimidate), the Adored (Devotion and Nurture), the Hardened (Discipline and Intimidate), the Beloved (Praise and Nurture), the Mirror (Mimicry). The stat benefits come from mastered paths. Check titles in both a game result and the portable Pokémon summary.

## Daycare Inn

**Entry and screens.** The Inn opens after Gym 1. `enterDaycare` branches between first tutorial/free Egg, permanent trade, and the after-hours scene. `#story-daycare-scene` shows receipts and the trade story. `#story-daycare-overlay` contains `#daycare-close-btn`, party choices, `data-daycare-drop`, optionally expanded stored Pokémon and the optional staff notice. Read the staff notice, cancel a selected trade, confirm a valid one, and follow each follow-up dialogue path. Favorite or protected members, Eggs and the last usable fighter have eligibility guards.

**Egg life cycle.** `daycare.freeEggClaimed` records the initial Grade 3 gift. Delivery uses Party or PC room. When both are full, the Matron holds the Egg for a later return. A separate permanent trade consumes one eligible Pokémon and creates an Egg in its place, using a legal stronger grade and favoring a shared type. The fallback retains an eligible hatch species when the selected generations limit the stronger pool. Current Eggs store `eggSpecies` and `eggLaidAtCity`. `_storyEggHatchCity` counts two town arrivals, clamped to the final authored hub. Older Eggs without a town record use the badge 7 hatch rule. The party and PC show pending or hatch-ready state. `#story-hatch-scene` plays the arrival reveal and identifies each destination. Hatch state preserves slot identity and ownership bookkeeping. Eggs occupy a physical slot and never enter battle.

**Hidden entrance.** The staff notice reads “On the second knock, wait.” After six badges the dialogue presents `Tell me` / `No thanks`, `Go down the stairs` / leave, and `Step inside` / `Go back upstairs`. Only stepping inside commits the `daycare.secretRevealed` and `pits.fightClubUnlocked` branch. After the campaign, the Crucible is the Fight Club entrance. Art and portraits change from the Inn and matron to the underground stairs and fight referee. ## Fight Club and Pits

**Roster and rounds.** The six-badge Inn route opens the story bracket. The story branch requires six usable fighters. Crucible repeats require three from Party and at least one additional fighter in Party or PC. `#story-pits-overlay` / `data-pits-pick` show ordered, cancellable choices. `#pits-start-btn` confirms the locked trio. `#story-pits-draft-overlay` previews the house's three counter-picks and offers the other three in House reserves. The five rounds use Basic Trainer, three Elites and a Gym Leader-class final. The house's six are drawn with current run generation restrictions and class/progression/difficulty grades and power tiers. The same trio is fielded and healed before every round.

**Outcomes.** `#story-pits-defeat-overlay` exposes free retry and a separately confirmed permanent forfeit. Retry reuses the locked opponent trio. The usual Story refund restores spent Bag items on a loss. Forfeit removes the three selected fighters, writes Memorial records and can promote a PC reserve if Party would otherwise have no usable fighter. Opposing defeated Pokémon can also be recorded in Memorial. Check cancelling the confirmation, pending-save focus, name disclosure and promotion when the remaining slots contain only Eggs. Story win `#story-pits-win` pays half the next Gym Leader's purse (at least 1,000G) and +2 permanent bonus points to each stat per round, capped at +10, for all eligible party members. Crucible repeat `#story-pits-result` offers the gold payout. The story bracket closes after its reward. Visit streak, best streak and payout counters live in `pits`.

**Recovery.** `_pitsStartBracket` rolls and heals against a temporary state, then commits the first house draft before leaving selection. A denied write keeps the same selection, party condition and random state on `#story-pits-overlay`. Once underway, the round and chosen opponent are in `pits` until settlement. Reloading an interrupted bracket restores the original party instead of forcing a partial permanent loss. Successful forfeit and win store their outcome before showing the result. ## Game Corner

**Entry and screens.** `enterCasino` mounts `#screen-story-casino`, using the Game Corner venue in Glasswater City or The Crucible. `#story-casino-tab-flip`, `#story-casino-tab-slots` and `#story-casino-tab-roulette` control lazy game panels. Each table has an expandable rule/payout panel. `#story-casino-stats-btn` opens table and lifetime stats. Back returns to the originating hub. Check the transition at the start/end of an animation, a low bankroll, and reopening or switching tabs.

| Table | Player controls and rules | State to inspect after reload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coin Flip `flip` | Pick Heads or Tails, stake at least 50G and Flip. Win chance is 49% and a successful call pays twice the bet. Same-side streaks affect prize tier. | Committed result, streak and lifetime table statistics |
| Slots `slots` | 50/100/200G, Spin, three reels, paytable and optional reel hold during a free re-spin. The re-spin uses the stake tier that earned it. | Last result and held symbol, `casinoSlotsReplay` and no second debit on a free re-spin |
| Poké Roulette `roulette` | 12 cells, choose and remove chips, Undo/Clear/Repeat. A direct hit pays 11× the winning chip. Success is based on the net after the full stake. | Actual paid cells, net receipt and prize, and repeat bet choice as supported by the active table |

The Lucky Chip `casinoChip500` gives 500G once when redeemed. All bets use run Gold. `casinoStats`, `casinoLastResults`, `casinoSlotsReplay` and the run random source underpin recovery. Gift, stake, result and prize settle durably before the reveal animation can be interrupted. Verify 12 bets on all cells charge a net loss even when a cell lights up. Check the Story music, per-table animation/SFX, reduced motion and optional long rules at the same time. ## Wild and legendary captures

**Entry paths.** The first wild tutorial follows the early Rival sequence and introduces the ball/leave choice. Optional encounters open from route progression, Wander/grass, Camp, Safari and Crucible Wild Encounter. `#screen-story-catch` / `#story-catch-body` host the common sprite, encounter caption, probability/ball choice and result. Every entry supplies its own callback so leaving or completing the report returns to the correct road, camp, session or hub. Ordinary throws select Poké/Great/Ultra/Master via `data-ball-type`. `#story-catch-run-btn` handles leaving where used. `data-catch-continue` completes a committed encounter and `data-dex-name` opens the exact captured species/form entry.

**Result states.** Check a successful throw, miss and flee, empty inventory, final ball, Master Ball, interruption during throw, shiny/form sprites, and each placement branch. Full Party offers placement or replacement where available and stores surplus in PC when supported. The capture receipt `catchRecovery` carries the encounter through placement and the Pokédex jump back, preserving the one awarded creature, origin markers and spent ball. Pokédex seen/caught data belongs in both run collection and cumulative records with the appropriate scope.

**Roaming sighting.** `Gym Leader 8` queues one legal sub-legendary species that is absent from Party and PC, keyed as `roamingLegendary.pending` / `gym8`. It interrupts the next eligible Story battle and leaves the Mystery Figure fight to its own scene. Check the initial Ranger hint, sighting veil/name reveal, audio, shiny/form art, one-throw warning, Master Ball, and the two distinct exits. Returning to town allows preparation and preserves the same pending species. Confirming departure or throwing the only ball spends the attempt and records the outcome. Interrupted capture receipts and committed outcome records prevent spending or awarding twice. Gen restrictions can leave no eligible sighting. `SUB_LEGENDARY_POOL`, `_ROAMING_TRIGGERS` and `data/story/ball-math.json` set the species/trigger and the shared ball multipliers, grade rates and flee rates. ## Safari Zone

**Entry and actions.** `enterSafariZone` is available in City 5 after four badges and from The Crucible. First admission is free for the current run. Later entry asks to spend 10,000G and warns that unused Safari Balls expire. A visit provides 10 Safari Balls and at most 10 encounters. The session has its own ball stock. In `#screen-story-catch` the Safari card exposes Safari Ball, Bait, Rock, Next encounter/Skip and Leave Safari. Bait reduces catch and post-miss flee chances. Rock increases both. Bait and Rock also roll on use with a separate capped flee chance that depends on the Rock count and the action chosen. Each modifier stacks at most three times for the current encounter. New encounters reset those counters.

## Battle Frontier

The hub `#screen-story-frontier` from The Crucible shows two distinct series, with their own start, rules, result and record. `#story-frontier-body` lays out the current series so an active challenge can be resumed before selecting another. Back returns to The Crucible.

**Endless ladder.** `frontierStartRun` / `frontierStartNext` open six-on-six rounds. Opposing grades rise with the streak. Opponent scaling tops out at ×3 HP and ×2 Defense, Sp. Def and Speed. The rules disclosure states the opener's current scaling and offers **End run and record streak** while active. A win permits the next round. A loss or confirmed end banks the streak and resets the run. `frontier.currentStreak`, `.currentRound`, `.active`, `.highscores` and `.lastLadderResult` persist. `#frontier-ladder-receipt` reports clear, end, personal best or a pending write with a Save result button. Read the Top 10 streaks disclosure and check round 1, continuation, finish and write retry.

**Seeded challenges.** The three authored cards are Quarry Trials (`frontier-quarry`, Quarry crest), Lakeside Circuit (`frontier-lakeside`, Glasswater ferry stamp) and Plateau Three (`frontier-plateau`, Plateau matchbook). Each card has its own version, seed and three opponent themes. Opening the card/review shows the proposed team, rules and challenge record before committing. The roster, allowed generations, mechanics and opponent sets remain locked for the attempt. Each round starts with a fresh roster and honors held items. The state machine uses `ready`, `in-progress`, `cleared` and `ended`. Loss or confirmed quit closes the attempt. The player chooses when to try again. Outcomes keep `lastCommittedResult` and the profile keeps `bestRound` plus the first completion date per card/revision. Frontier cards and the endless ladder have separate records. Exercise acceptance and cancellation, review, confirm next round, loss, clear, quit, reload, save retry and generation-legal teams.
