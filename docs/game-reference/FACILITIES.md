# Town and Crucible facilities

Use the stable action keys below to find a facility in the live city menu, the Notebook directory, or the Crucible. The numbered city codes are implementation anchors. The town names stay fixed while the Gym Leader and some local dialogue can vary by run. The runtime rows, availability conditions, and loaded data define what the player can actually use. ### Shared presentation and states

- The city objective is separate from other actions. Open Shop, Training, Recover, or the local story/errand group to verify discovery, useful prices, “New” or “Upgraded”, disabled reasons and focus after returning. Guide portraits, dialogue panels, notification hints and optional scenes have their own entry flags. The two facility gate keys are Mart in Pallet where the Mart is listed, and Center in C1 if unseen. Tutor tier-ups and optional facility lessons surface as invites. ## Shops, Relics and field tools

### `shops` · `mart`, `dept`, `stoneShop`

- **Pokémart and Department Store** share `#screen-story-shop` with a distinct store name, portrait and current stock. Read the featured and regular supply groups, item icon, explanation, quantity and cost, then buy into the Poké Ball or item inventory. The Mart welcome gives five Poké Balls. The Department welcome gives a Great Ball and a Revive. Featured stock can sell out for the town. Check stock, affordability, purchase and any one-shot welcome award, returning cash, battle consumable rules, and item-disabled run settings. The Mart usually supplies balls and medicine. The Department Store adds higher-tier gear and rarer supplies. Easy and Very Easy see Department at the beginning. The source catalog defines 16 Mart entries and 14 Department-specific entries. The Department uses both lists and rotates additional featured stock. - **Stones and Beyond** uses `#screen-story-stone-shop`. Its 24 items cover ten elemental stones and fourteen other evolution items. It shows carried counts, flat 500G charge, confirmation, Stonewise Token route and disabled entries. Check species or generation eligibility separately in Evolution Tutor. ### `relics` · `relic`

The stock and type variations are persisted per town and one claim is allowed per town stock. Manage can be visited between Browse locations. Check the current effect and scope (both sides or player's side), first keeper scene, unique offer, already owned and depleted states, management tabs, enable/disable, persistence and combat effect. Since the Crucible uses the League city row, test its offers against the existing C9 claim. ## Training and long-term team investment

### `move-tutor` · `tutor`

- **Opening and identity.** The Tutor is available in Pallet after starter selection. The shared `#screen-story-tutor` screen opens in Moves mode. The partner switcher, isolated info action, equipped move slots, quick suggestions, search, filters and sort belong to the active partner. Advanced details explain move damage category, power, accuracy and legality. Check empty, filtered-out, equipped, locked, replacement, deletion with at least one move retained, confirmed and cancelled states. Species, current moves, role, difficulty/unlocks and native move legality drive recommendation eligibility. [Move Tutor entry and picker](../../battle.html#L81364) · [Filter composition](../../tests/suites/story-tutor-filter-compose.test.js) · [Sort](../../tests/suites/story-tutor-sort.test.js) · [Stage and lock checks](../../tests/suites/story-tutor-staged-pool.test.js)
- **Pacing and learning costs.** Current shared city tiers for C0 through C7 are **1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5** and stay at 5 afterward. The starter bond permits one extra tier, capped at 5. Tier evaluates damage and utility, including priority, multi-hit damage, healing, setup and status effects. “Natural”, “Learnt” and “Awakened” describe the learning source and charge 1,000G, 2,500G and 5,000G respectively. An eligible Heart Scale pays for one lesson. Tutor ranks C0/C3/C7 control ceremony and reward. The shared tier clock controls permit. Check that the town’s unlock card and direct Tutor link reflect the same availability. - **Hidden Power.** The Diviner’s road encounter or arrival at C3 unlocks the lesson. Review the natural type derived from IVs, the choice to keep it, alternative type options and the separately saved override. One favor per run can set an alternative type through a paid lesson or retune a known copy. Opening, cancelling or keeping the natural type preserves the favor. Compare the saved type with the move details, Mentor and battle result, including older saves. ### `battle-dojo` · `dojo`

C1 onward. The shared training screen opens in Loadout mode with held items and ability tabs. Each has recommendations, searchable options, option details, current selection, preview and a separate confirm. White Belt C1 permits natural abilities and foundation items. Black Belt C4 opens most Hidden Abilities and tier-2 staple items. Master C6 opens tougher Hidden Abilities and remaining held items. Grandmaster C8 opens the custom species-specific Awakened pool. Ability costs are 2,000G natural, 3,000G Hidden and 5,000G Awakened. Ordinary held-item tiers cost 500G, 1,000G or 2,500G. An Ability Capsule pays for an eligible ability, an Emblem of Honor for an eligible held item. Check unavailable items, run settings, form-specific stones/crystals, incompatible slot replacement, removing a held item, and the battle engine’s staged foe ceiling against the same gates. ### `battle-mentor` · `mentor`

Recurring from Pallet with a battle-ready partner. `#screen-story-mentor` previews an affordable and legal build and lets the player review or select individual parts before purchase. The general suggestion chain also feeds the city preparation action and the Notebook's My team shortcut. The mentor's conditional options follow local facilities, battle-form eligibility, the no-items run, actual budget and roster. ### `iv-trainer` · `fanclub`

The Fan Club Chairman and six-stat IV training screen are available C1 onward. First entry supplies one of each vitamin. Compare HP Up, Protein, Iron, Calcium, Zinc and Carbos stock with Bag. Training increases a corresponding IV by up to 3 and caps at 31. Lowering reduces by up to 3, observes a floor of 0, prompts before committing and gives no vitamin in return. Showing effective Fight Club bonuses alongside IVs helps distinguish both. The Fan Club focuses on party partners. The separate Bag picker can select eligible stored partners and returns to the vitamin on escape or apply. Check party/stored, no vitamins, capped IV, zero floor, confirmation, maxed stats and persistence. ### `battle-forms` · `colress`

Colress opens from C7 when the run has unlocked forms. `#screen-story-colress` shows the current standardized or assigned form, recommended alternatives, all allowed options and the ineligible state. Compare Mega, Z-Move, Dynamax, Gigantamax and Tera assignment, correct stones/crystals, compatible species, Tera type choice, preview, removal to standard and exact charge. Training costs 5,000G or a Wishing Piece. The mode's mechanism ceiling is separate from a Pokémon’s assignment and should be tested in combat on both sides. Experience Share is a separate Bag-applied level upgrade granted by eligible flagship raid outcomes. The usual battle level is 50. It awards six uses per eligible event, with a cap of +3 for each Pokémon. Check the raid award, wallet and selected partner's battle stats, including PC eligibility and unsuccessful applications. ### `evolution` · `evolab`

The Evolution Tutor and Stonewise Granny show eligible party partners, target form previews, routes to Stones and Beyond and advancement locks. C2/C3 gates require an admitted stage and G3/G4 target. ### `cable-link` · `link`

Bill's Cable Link opens in C2. Same-grade exchange replaces a Pokémon with a new roll, rebuild rerolls the selected species' build, and upgrade trades for a stronger grade if the source is below G1 and the eligible species pool exists. Starting G1/G2/G3/G4 exchange costs 14,000G/12,000G/9,000G/6,000G, G4/G3/G2 upgrades cost 9,000G/13,000G/22,000G, and rebuild costs 5,000G. The first same-grade exchange on a casual difficulty gets a 22% reduction before applying any voucher. Bill’s introduction grants a voucher for half off one order. Swaps and upgrades permanently replace a partner, so favorites and story-locked partners have both Gold and voucher trade buttons disabled. Same-species rebuild preserves the partner’s identity, IVs, earned levels, stat bonuses and Camp bond. Preview the available grade/generation pool, check protected and ordinary partners, then verify RNG after cancelling, failure or retry. Cable Link is a specific early-roll exception for run-enabled battle forms.
