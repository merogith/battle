# Puny World Overworld

Shade’s 432 × 1040 pixel atlas is under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The staged copy is source art for the Journey Atlas terrain and wayfinding prototype. Keep the game’s own route names and landmarks as the visible labels. The author lists the PNG as `punyworld-overworld-tileset.png`.
